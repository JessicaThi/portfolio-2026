Context

One client, three projects

Dotworld is a company based in Lausanne, Switzerland, that launches several web services. I started working with them in March 2021, when I began working as a freelancer.

I worked on CV Ninja (creating a CV online), Twenty Pay (paying the bill at a restaurant) and Rezilli (cancelling a subscription).

My role: building the mockups into responsive interfaces, for desktop and mobile, with Vue.js or Nuxt.js and Tailwind.css.

CV Ninja · 2021

Creating a CV online

A site to fill in a CV step by step, choose a template and download it.

  • TemplatesFive CV templates (Bordeaux, Lyon, Nantes, Nice, Toulouse): built from the mockups, color variants, finishing touches. The name size adapts to its length, and long email addresses wrap to the next line.
  • SiteA few small changes on the rest of the site.

The same fictional CV in each template, displayed locally. Click a CV to enlarge it.

Twenty Pay · 2021 – 2022

Paying the bill at a restaurant

You scan a QR code on the table, see the bill and pay from your phone.

  • AppThe screens of the user flow: menu, dishes, summary, bill splitting, payment, review and thank-you page.
  • SiteThe promotional site for restaurants: demo sections with video, benefits, reviews, FAQ and contact; fixed menu, mobile layout, legal pages.

What I learned

Building for real data

On the CV Ninja templates, the layout had to hold up whatever the content: a very long name, an email address that overflows. I learned to build for real data, not only for the mockup.
